**MANAGED IT SERVICES AGREEMENT**

This Managed IT Services Agreement (this "Agreement") is dated as of January 2, 2024, and is entered into by and between **[Family Office LLC]**, a [State] limited liability company ("Client"), and **[Provider], Inc.**, a [State] corporation ("Provider"). Client and Provider are each a "party" and together the "parties."

**RECITALS**

A. Provider is in the business of providing managed information technology services, including help desk support, device management, network monitoring, backup administration and related professional services.

B. Client wishes to engage Provider to provide such services for Client's office, and Provider wishes to provide them, on the terms and subject to the conditions set out below.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out in this Agreement and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are acknowledged, the parties agree as follows.

**1. DEFINITIONS**

1.1 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday, or a day on which commercial banks in [City], [State] are authorized or required by law to close.

1.2 "Covered Location" means each location listed as a Covered Location in Schedule A.

1.3 "Covered User" means each individual employee or contractor of Client listed by Client in the onboarding record maintained under Section 4.2.

1.4 "Effective Date" means the date on which this Agreement has been signed by both parties, being the later of the two dates shown beneath the parties' signatures below.

1.5 "Fees" means the fees set out in Schedule A, as adjusted from time to time in accordance with this Agreement.

1.6 "Initial Term" has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

1.7 "Renewal Term" has the meaning given in Section 3.2.

1.8 "Services" means the services described in Schedule A and any additional services the parties agree in writing.

1.9 "Term" means the Initial Term together with each Renewal Term, if any.

**2. SERVICES**

2.1 *Provision of Services.* Provider shall provide the Services to Client at each Covered Location and remotely to each Covered User, in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted industry practices.

2.2 *Personnel.* Provider shall assign suitably qualified personnel to perform the Services. Provider may change personnel assigned to Client at its discretion, provided that it gives Client reasonable notice of any change to Client's designated account manager.

2.3 *Subcontractors.* Provider may use subcontractors to perform portions of the Services, provided that Provider remains responsible for the performance of its subcontractors as for its own performance.

2.4 *Changes to Services.* Either party may request a change to the Services. No change is binding unless set out in a written amendment signed by both parties.

**3. TERM AND RENEWAL**

3.1 *Initial Term.* This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of thirty-six (36) months (the "Initial Term"), unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 12.

3.2 *Renewal.* Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive renewal terms of twelve (12) months each (each, a "Renewal Term"), unless either party delivers written notice of non-renewal to the other party not less than sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of the then-current Term.

3.3 *Effect of Expiration.* Upon expiration or termination of this Agreement, Sections 7, 8, 10, 11, 12.3 and 15 survive.

**4. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES**

4.1 Client shall provide Provider with reasonable access to Covered Locations during Client's normal business hours and at other times agreed in advance.

4.2 Client shall maintain, and provide to Provider on request, an onboarding record listing each Covered User and each device to be supported, and shall notify Provider of additions and removals within five (5) Business Days.

4.3 Client is responsible for the accuracy of information it provides and for obtaining any third-party consents needed for Provider to access Client systems.

**5. FEES AND PAYMENT**

5.1 *Fees.* Client shall pay Provider the Fees set out in Schedule A. Recurring Fees are invoiced monthly in advance on the first day of each calendar month.

5.2 *Invoices.* Invoices are payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Client may dispute any invoiced amount in good faith by written notice describing the dispute, and the parties shall work to resolve it promptly.

5.3 *Taxes.* Fees are exclusive of sales, use and similar taxes, which Client shall pay where applicable, other than taxes on Provider's income.

5.4 *Adjustment of Fees.* Provider may increase the Fees effective as of the first day of any Renewal Term by giving Client written notice not less than sixty (60) days before that Renewal Term begins. Any such increase shall not exceed the lesser of (a) the percentage increase in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers (CPI-U) for the twelve (12) months ending with the most recent month for which that index has been published at the date of the notice, and (b) four percent (4%). The Fees shall not otherwise be increased during the Term except by a written amendment signed by both parties.

5.5 *Late Payment.* Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less.

**6. SERVICE LEVELS**

6.1 Provider shall meet the response times set out in Schedule A for help desk requests submitted through Provider's support channels.

6.2 If Provider fails to meet the response-time target for priority-one requests in any calendar month, Client is entitled to a service credit of five percent (5%) of that month's recurring Fees, which is Client's sole monetary remedy for that failure.

**7. CONFIDENTIALITY**

7.1 Each party shall hold the other party's confidential information in confidence, use it only to perform or receive the Services, and disclose it only to personnel and advisers who need to know it and are bound by obligations of confidentiality at least as protective as this Section.

7.2 These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving party, was known to it before disclosure, is independently developed, or is required to be disclosed by law, provided that the receiving party gives prompt notice where lawful.

**8. CLIENT DATA**

8.1 As between the parties, Client owns all data that Client or its Covered Users store on or transmit through systems managed by Provider ("Client Data").

8.2 Provider shall access Client Data only as needed to perform the Services and shall maintain reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards for Client Data in its possession.

8.3 Provider shall notify Client without undue delay after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to Client Data in Provider's possession.

**9. WARRANTIES**

9.1 Each party warrants that it has the authority to enter into this Agreement.

9.2 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET OUT IN THIS AGREEMENT, PROVIDER MAKES NO WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY IMPLIED WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

**10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY**

10.1 EXCEPT FOR BREACH OF SECTION 7 OR SECTION 8, NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES.

10.2 EXCEPT FOR BREACH OF SECTION 7 OR SECTION 8, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL LIABILITY UNDER THIS AGREEMENT IS LIMITED TO THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS BEFORE THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM.

**11. INDEMNIFICATION**

11.1 Each party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims arising from its gross negligence or willful misconduct in connection with this Agreement.

**12. TERMINATION**

12.1 *For Cause.* Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice if the other party materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice describing it.

12.2 *Early Termination by Client.* Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience on ninety (90) days' written notice, subject to payment of an early termination fee equal to fifty percent (50%) of the recurring monthly Fees that would have been payable for the remainder of the then-current Term after the effective date of termination.

12.3 *Transition Assistance.* On expiration or termination, Provider shall provide reasonable transition assistance for up to sixty (60) days at the hourly rate in Schedule A.

**13. INSURANCE**

13.1 Provider shall maintain commercial general liability, professional liability (errors and omissions) and cyber liability insurance in amounts customary for providers of similar services, and shall provide certificates of insurance on request.

**14. NOTICES**

14.1 *Form of Notice.* All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by nationally recognized overnight courier, or by certified mail, return receipt requested, to the address set out in Section 14.2 (or such other address as a party designates by notice under this Section). A notice is effective upon receipt. Notices by email are not effective for purposes of Sections 3, 5.4, 12 or 14, but email may be used for routine operational communications.

14.2 *Addresses.*

If to Client: [Family Office LLC], Attn: Chief Operating Officer, [Client Street Address], [City], [State].

If to Provider: [Provider], Inc., Attn: Legal Department, 400 [Street A], [City], [State].

**15. GENERAL**

15.1 *Entire Agreement.* This Agreement, including its Schedules and any amendments, is the entire agreement of the parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior proposals and understandings.

15.2 *Amendment.* This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties. In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and an amendment, the amendment controls; as between amendments, the later-dated amendment controls.

15.3 *Assignment.* Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, except to a successor to all or substantially all of its business.

15.4 *Waiver.* No failure or delay in exercising a right operates as a waiver of it.

15.5 *Severability.* If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder of this Agreement continues in effect.

15.6 *Governing Law.* This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of [State], without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules.

15.7 *Counterparts.* This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is an original and all of which together are one instrument.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have signed this Agreement as of the dates set out below.
