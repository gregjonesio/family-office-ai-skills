# Sample input: Capital Call Notice Check

A fictional capital call. Every figure, date and identifier is invented; names are placeholders. These are the same four documents as the [test kit](../test-kit/), whose [answer key](../test-kit/answer-key.md) lists what a careful operator should find.

## Request

> "Using the capital-call-notice-check skill, check this capital call notice against the previous one and our record of the fund's terms."
