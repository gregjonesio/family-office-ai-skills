# Answer key

Written together with the test documents, before any run, and withheld from every session that was tested. Keep it away from the model you are testing: give the AI only the skill and documents 1 to 3.

This key is a benchmark, not a complete list of every difference. It scores nine planted changes the letter does not mention. A good comparison will also list the other differences on the pages (below); they are real, but they are not scored.

## What the cover letter mentions (3)

- Premium: $41,200 to $44,900 (about 9%).
- Dwelling limit (Coverage A): $6,200,000 to $6,510,000, under the 5% inflation guard.
- All other perils deductible: $10,000 to $25,000.

Other differences on the pages, outside the benchmark and not scored: other structures and contents rise 5% (in step with Coverage A); the policy number suffix and the policy period roll forward; each section's premium changes; the forms list gains PC-290.

## What changed on the pages but is not in the letter (9)

1. Extended replacement cost: 150% to 125% of Coverage A.
2. Loss of use: no time limit to a 24-month limit.
3. Windstorm or hail deductible (including named storm): $10,000 flat to 2% of Coverage A, shown as $130,200, about 13 times larger.
4. Water backup and sump overflow: full Coverage A limit to a $50,000 limit.
5. Ordinance or law: 100% to 25% of Coverage A.
6. A new protective device condition (PC-290): theft coverage applies only if the central station burglar alarm is in working order and set whenever the house is unoccupied.
7. Jewelry valuation basis: agreed value to stated value.
8. Jewelry schedule: the $38,000 wristwatch is removed; the schedule total falls from $480,000 to $442,000 and the remaining items are renumbered.
9. Main policy form PCH-100: edition 04/23 to 04/25. The wording may have changed; the declarations cannot show how.

## A statement worth testing

The letter says jewelry coverage continues on renewal. That is true in the narrow sense, but the schedule lost an item and the valuation basis changed.

## Scoring rule

- **Found:** the output names the specific item and the direction of the change.
- **Missed:** the item is absent, or named without its direction.
- **Error:** anything the output states that the documents do not support, including a wrong figure.
- Also note, without scoring: any question to the broker that the documents already answer, and anything true the output found that is not on this key (confirm it from the documents before crediting it).
