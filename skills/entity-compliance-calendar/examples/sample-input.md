**About your next annual report**

Every domestic limited liability company must file an annual report and pay the annual tax each year. The annual report is due on or before the last day of the month in which the company was formed (its anniversary month). The annual tax amount is shown in your online filing account and is payable with the report.

A company that fails to file its annual report and pay the annual tax by the due date is subject to a late penalty, and a company that remains delinquent may be administratively dissolved after notice.
