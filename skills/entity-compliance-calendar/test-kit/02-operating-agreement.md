**LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANY AGREEMENT**
**OF**
**[ENTITY A] HOLDINGS LLC**

This Limited Liability Company Agreement (this "Agreement") is dated as of July 1, 2023, and is entered into by the persons listed on Schedule A as Members.

**ARTICLE 1. DEFINITIONS**

1.1 "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a day on which banks in [City C], [State A] are authorized or required to close.

1.2 "Code" means the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended.

1.3 "Effective Date" means the date on which the Certificate of Formation of the Company is filed with the Secretary of State of [State A].

1.4 "Fiscal Year" means the fiscal year of the Company, which shall end on June 30 of each year.

1.5 "Fiscal Quarter" means each period of three months ending on September 30, December 31, March 31 and June 30.

1.6 "Majority Interest" means Members holding more than fifty percent of the Percentage Interests then held by all Members.

1.7 "Taxable Year" means the taxable year of the Company for federal income tax purposes, which shall be the calendar year unless otherwise required by the Code.

*[Sections 1.8 to 1.22 omitted.]*

**ARTICLE 2. FORMATION**

2.1 The Company was formed as a limited liability company under the laws of [State A] by the filing of its Certificate of Formation. The rights and obligations of the Members shall be as provided in this Agreement, which shall be effective as of the Effective Date.

2.2 The Manager shall cause the Company to maintain its existence and good standing in [State A] and to be registered or qualified to do business in each other jurisdiction in which such registration is required, and shall cause all reports required of the Company by any such jurisdiction to be filed when due.

*[Articles 3 to 5 omitted: purpose, capital contributions, allocations and distributions.]*

**ARTICLE 6. MANAGEMENT**

6.1 **Manager.** The business and affairs of the Company shall be managed by a Manager. [Manager A] is hereby appointed as the Manager for a term ending on the third anniversary of the Effective Date. Upon the expiration of such term, the Manager may be reappointed for a further term of three years by the written consent of Members holding a Majority Interest.

6.2 **Removal.** The Manager may be removed at any time, with or without cause, by the written consent of Members holding a Majority Interest.

*[Sections 6.3 to 6.9 omitted.]*

**ARTICLE 7. MEETINGS OF MEMBERS**

7.1 **Annual meeting.** An annual meeting of the Members shall be held within ninety (90) days after the end of each Fiscal Year, at a time and place determined by the Manager, to receive the Manager's report on the Fiscal Year.

7.2 **Notice.** Written notice of each meeting of Members, stating its date, time and place, shall be delivered to each Member not less than ten (10) nor more than sixty (60) days before the meeting.

7.3 **Action without a meeting.** Any action that may be taken at a meeting of Members may be taken without a meeting by written consent of Members holding not less than the Percentage Interests required to take such action at a meeting.

**ARTICLE 8. BOOKS AND RECORDS**

8.1 The Manager shall keep complete and accurate books of account of the Company at its principal office, together with minutes of all meetings of Members and all written consents.

**ARTICLE 9. REPORTS TO MEMBERS**

9.1 **Quarterly statements.** Within forty-five (45) days after the end of each Fiscal Quarter, the Manager shall deliver to each Member a statement of such Member's capital account as of the end of that Fiscal Quarter.

9.2 **Annual financial statements.** Within one hundred twenty (120) days after the end of each Fiscal Year, the Manager shall deliver to each Member the financial statements of the Company for that Fiscal Year.

9.3 **Tax information.** Within seventy-five (75) days after the end of each Taxable Year, the Manager shall deliver to each Member such information as is necessary for the preparation of that Member's federal and state income tax returns.
