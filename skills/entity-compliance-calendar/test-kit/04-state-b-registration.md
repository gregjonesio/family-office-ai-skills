## Part 2: Notice to registered foreign entities

**[STATE B] DEPARTMENT OF STATE: Corporations Bureau**
**Annual report requirement**

Each foreign limited liability company registered in [State B] must file an annual report during the filing period of **January 1 through April 15** of each year, beginning with the year after the year of registration. The report confirms the company's registered agent, principal office address and managers.

A report filed after April 15 is subject to a late fee. A registration for which no report has been filed by the following June 30 may be revoked after notice.

Reports are filed online through the Bureau's business portal using the entity's ID and filing PIN.

*This notice is general information and is sent to all registered foreign entities.*
